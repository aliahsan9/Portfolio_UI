BusinessOS

One platform. Connected business operations.

BusinessOS is an AI-powered business management platform designed to help small and growing businesses manage inventory, customers, orders, and financial operations through a centralized workspace.

Built with Angular, ASP.NET Core, SQL Server, and a multi-tenant architecture designed with maintainability, security, and future scalability in mind.

02 / THE SOLUTION

A connected workspace for running a business.

BusinessOS connects essential business workflows through a shared application and data model. Instead of treating inventory, customers, and orders as isolated features, the platform brings them together in one operational system.

The goal is not simply to display information on a dashboard. It is to provide the underlying workflows, APIs, data relationships, and access controls required to manage business operations consistently.
