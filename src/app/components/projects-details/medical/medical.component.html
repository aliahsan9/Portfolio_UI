About The Project

This Medical ERP system was developed to modernize pharmacy and healthcare operations through a centralized digital platform.

The platform manages medicines, suppliers, purchases, sales, invoices and inventory tracking while maintaining scalability and clean architecture principles.

Strong focus was placed on performance, security, responsive design and enterprise-grade user experience.
